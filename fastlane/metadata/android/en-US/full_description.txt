occm is a dedicated Mastodon client built to make using the occm server as smooth as possible.

Log in directly with your occm account and enjoy the core Mastodon experience - home timeline, notifications, posting, replies, boosts, favorites, and custom emoji - without any extra setup.

Key features:

- Quick login: sign in with your occm account in just a few taps and start browsing right away.
- Full SNS features: home timeline, notifications, search, and profiles, plus posting, replies, boosts, favorites, and custom emoji, all working smoothly.
- Fast DM access: the direct message screen has been tuned for quick back-and-forth conversations. New messages show up right away without needing to refresh, and avatars and custom emoji render smoothly in the conversation.
- Tuned for occm: unlike general-purpose Mastodon apps, this client is adjusted specifically for the occm server, so it integrates more reliably with its behavior.

If you're an occm user, this app lets you go from checking your timeline to chatting in real time, all in one place.
